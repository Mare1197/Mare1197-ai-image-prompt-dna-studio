## Vehicle DNA

## Modification categories
Light, moderate, heavy, drift-tuned, track-tuned, street-tuned, widebody, show car, custom luxury, off-road, futuristic and concept. “Heavily modified” chooses a coherent subset rather than every feature. “Drift-tuned” prioritizes low stance, steering angle, rear tires, drift wheels, aero, smoke, livery, cooling, cage/tow/quick-release details as appropriate.

## Placement

## Layering
Paint, wrap, livery and decals are independent layers. Variants can change one layer only. Exact locks preserve each layer and placement across views.
