## 02 Effect Bank

- Optical/lens: bloom, halation, diffusion, flare, prism, chromatic aberration, RGB split, bokeh, shallow/deep focus, tilt-shift, vignette, radial/zoom blur.
- Film/analog: fine/heavy grain, scratches, dust, gate weave, light leaks, faded print, lifted/crushed blacks, cross-processing, expired-film shifts, date stamp, soft roll-off.
- Broadcast/digital texture: VHS/CRT scanlines, tracking noise, color bleed, interlacing, static, timecode, datamosh, compression, frame echo, RGB delay.
- Lighting: rim/double-rim, backlight, silhouette, high/low key, chiaroscuro, split, Rembrandt, under/top/side/cross light, practicals, headlights, club/strobe/laser, volumetric rays, caustics, gobo, warm-cool and colored contrast.
- Atmosphere/weather: mist, fog, smoke, tire smoke, steam, dust, ash, embers, rain, wet surfaces, puddles, snow, frost, lightning, wind, leaves, petals, heat haze.
- Motion: panning/background/radial blur, long-exposure trails, tire/wheel blur, speed lines, fabric/hair motion, particles, debris, frozen motion, stroboscopic motion, whip-pan, parallax.
- Surfaces: wet road/floor, mirrors, chrome/black chrome, metallic flakes, pearlescent/iridescent paint, holographic film, oil slick, glass, polished concrete, carbon, vinyl, condensation, satin/silk/velvet response.
- Holographic/digital: AR/HUD panels, wireframe/blueprint/point cloud, projection mapping, grids, pixel sorting, glitch displacement, data fragments, polygon/voxel/particle dissolution, neon outlines and spectral layers.
- Surreal: levitation, floating objects, portals, folding space, impossible reflections, scale change, liquid surfaces, crystal/floral growth, alternate reflections and environment emerging from subjects.
- Graphic/print: halftone, duotone, posterization, screen print, risograph, collage, photocopy, graffiti, paint/ink splashes, technical annotations, magazine framing, paper and foil textures.
- Grading/detail: teal-orange, red-cyan, pink-blue, muted/pastel/candy, split tone, infrared/thermal, microtexture, fabric weave, skin pores, accurate wheels/reflections, global illumination, contact shadows and premium retouching.
