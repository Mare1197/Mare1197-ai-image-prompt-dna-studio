# Characters, Wardrobe and Monsters

## Human DNA
Role, adult age range, presentation, height/build/proportions, face, skin, hair, eyes, makeup, expression, wardrobe, footwear, accessories, pose, position, reference, consistency and locked/flexible traits.

## Human presets
Adult female/male/androgynous editorial model, driver, drift driver, crew member, lead/supporting fashion character, motorsport team, car-meet group, club group, luxury campaign character, technician, mechanic, designer and reference-derived character.

## Wardrobe
### Lingerie/editorial
Matching, lace, satin, minimal luxury, structured, corset/bustier tops, bodysuits, high-waisted and strappy sets, slip dresses, robes/transparent outer layers, jackets/blazers over lingerie, stockings, garter-inspired editorial detailing, gloves, chokers and jewelry.

### Skirts/dresses
Mini, pleated/fitted/leather/satin/technical/racing mini-skirts, wrap/pencil/asymmetric skirts, short fitted, bodycon, satin, cocktail and futuristic technical dresses.

### Tops/layers
Crop/racing/technical/fitted tops, tank, corset/bustier, satin, fitted long sleeve, racing/leather/technical jackets, bombers, blazers, transparent/reflective/holographic jackets and long coats.

### Bottoms
Fitted/leather/technical/racing/cargo/low-rise/tailored trousers and fitted/high-waisted/motorsport/denim/biker shorts.

## Allowed footwear
Stilettos, slim pumps, pointed/strappy/ankle-strap/slingback heels, slim heeled sandals and slim heeled ankle/knee/thigh boots. Vans low-tops, Old Skool/Authentic/Sk8-Hi-style shoes, flat canvas, slim skate/vulcanized/high-top/minimal leather and retro low-profile sneakers.

## Absolute exclusion
Platform, thick, chunky, dad, wedge, heavy lug, combat, exaggerated foam, bulky hiking and oversized sneaker soles. Invalid imports must be flagged. Randomization cannot select them.

## Creature DNA
Type, body structure, scale relative to humans/vehicles, surface, head/face, eyes/mouth/horns/ears/wings/tail/limbs/claws, palette/glow, clothing/accessories, pose/behavior/mood, environmental/vehicle interaction and locks.

## Creature categories
Humanoid demons, elegant/horned/shadow/pale/armored/synthetic/crystal/metallic/biomechanical humanoids; reptilian/dragon/kaiju/horned/quadruped/insectoid/mechanical/cosmic/serpentine giants; horned/gremlin/robot/alien/mini-dragon/spectral/slime/crystal/mechanical companions; gray/elegant/reptilian/insectoid/aquatic/translucent/bioluminescent/mechanical/cybernetic/plant aliens; ghost/shadow/smoke/energy/light/mirror/portal/masked/winged entities; androids, robots, holograms, avatars, wireframes, liquid metal and modular machines.

## Creature safeguards
Default to no graphic gore or exposed organs. Preserve readable anatomy and distinct silhouettes. Never merge human and creature bodies unless explicitly requesting a transformation concept.

## Pose/action bank
Human editorial, walking, leaning, sitting, crouching, group, jacket/helmet/key interactions; lingerie editorial standing/seated/reclined non-explicit/blazer/jacket/walking poses; vehicle parked/idling/rolling/accelerating/cornering/drifting/tandem/burnout/pit actions; monsters standing/crouching/emerging/floating/flying/coiling/protecting/posing/reflected/forming from particles.
